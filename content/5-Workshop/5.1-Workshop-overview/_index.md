### SmartDocAI Workshop Overview

This section provides a comprehensive overview of the hands-on workshop for building and deploying SmartDocAI - an AI-powered Document Knowledge Assistant running 100% on AWS Serverless Container Architecture.

The workshop guides learners step-by-step through deploying a battle-tested Enterprise RAG solution capable of extracting knowledge from long PDF/Word documents, delivering instant responses with source citations, and enforcing strict per-user multi-tenancy data isolation.
